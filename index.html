03 — One of my favourite memories

That day will always have a special place in my heart.

Earlier this year

There was a day earlier this year, the day before I came to Cape Town, that I don't think I'll ever forget.

I was looking after my aunt's place emkhonto, 'emzini wethu', and you came over to see me.

You were wearing a black dress.

It was my first time seeing you in a dress... and honestly, what a beautiful woman.

We ended up lying there on the bed, with your head resting on my chest.

Nothing extraordinary was happening. We weren't doing anything crazy. We were simply together.

But somehow, that became one of the best days of my life.

I remember you were a little stressed about school because VUT had denied you access to register. We were both worried about what was going to happen and what your next step would be.

Little did we know that only a few weeks later, the University of Mpumalanga would accept you and you would start a completely different chapter of your life.

Looking back now, that's probably one of the things I love most about that memory.

We had no idea what was coming next. We didn't know where life was going to take you. We were just there, together, lying on that bed, with your head on my chest, talking and worrying about school.

Just you and me, a black dress, your head on my chest, and a moment I wish I could experience one more time.

I don't think you realised how special that day was to me.

But it is one of those memories that I will always carry with me.

And whenever I think about it, I don't think about anything complicated.

I just remember you.
